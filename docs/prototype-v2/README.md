# Church Nominations Poll Prototype

Standalone, browser-local prototype for connected Administrator, Voter, and Presentation experiences. It is intentionally isolated from production code and APIs. The administrator manages the hierarchy Church → Election Term → Department → Position → Polls and Decisions.

## Run

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the Vite URL (normally `http://localhost:5173`). The administrator term workspace is `/admin/setup`; church settings are `/admin/setup/churches`; term management is `/admin/setup/terms`; a position's poll workspace is `/admin`. Stable voter and presentation views are `/vote` and `/presentation`; the compact toolbar on the position workspace provides copy, preview/open, and QR actions for these routes. Those actions never activate or change a poll. Browsing poll history changes only the admin's viewed poll; only an explicit **Open intake** or **Open Voting** action changes the active participant session, with a confirmation flow when another poll is open. Run `npm test` for workflow/model/state tests and `npm run build` for a production compile.

## Prototype behavior

- Fixtures include Elder and Treasurer positions, multiple openings, original nomination submissions with spelling variants, and a consolidated editable roster linked to its source submissions. The 2027 Church Elder is immediately explorable: Round 1 is closed with aggregate results and admin decisions, and Round 2 is open with Advanced candidates and sample anonymous ballots. Treasurer remains an intake example.
- The church setup fixture includes Riverside Seventh-day Adventist Church, a read-only historical 2026 term with election records, and a separately structured 2027 preparing term. The selected term is the primary workspace; church and term forms live in focused management views. Departments are expandable, and position editing/addition is available through contextual actions. Departments and positions are manually created per term; there are no templates, catalogs, imports, or copy-between-terms actions.
- Position setup supports editing the name, department, and openings without changing its stable ID or clearing existing polls, ballots, results, or decision history. Browsing a church, term, or position only changes administrator context. The active-session indicator and confirmation-backed switch are in the position election workspace; only that action changes the position shown in Voter and Presentation.
- Historical terms and their position workspaces are read-only; their polls, results, candidates, and decisions remain available for review.
- Intake may be closed for review; the administrator can edit candidate names, merge duplicate suggestions into one roster candidate, or exclude a candidate before approving the roster. Merging updates candidate source links and audit history without rewriting or deleting original nomination submissions.
- Nomination Polls and Voting Polls are explicit poll types in one history. Configure and save the maximum names per participant before opening nomination intake. The voter form enforces that cap per browser session, shows the remaining allowance, and retains each name under the nomination poll’s immutable original-submission snapshot. Close intake to review/consolidate and approve the roster. A Voting Poll can be created from that approved roster, from existing candidates, or directly from a manually entered candidate list when no nomination poll is needed.
- The administrator position election workspace presents a compact position summary, a permanent compact session/share toolbar, a short-label poll navigator, and one selected-poll workspace. Position-wide candidate status is available in a separate Candidates view. Selecting historical polls only changes the admin view. Opening a nomination or voting poll explicitly activates it for the voter/presentation session; if another poll is open, the admin must choose to keep it open or close it before switching.
- Current candidate statuses are derived from append-only, poll-linked decision events. Revising a decision supersedes the earlier current status without deleting its original poll decision or changing ballots/results. Historical poll views show their own decisions; the position header summarizes current effective statuses.
- The 2027 Elder fixture includes an approved Nomination Poll with seven immutable original submissions before its two Voting Polls. Treasurer remains a no-poll position for testing the explicit start choices and nomination lifecycle.
- Voting Results show live aggregate totals to the administrator while voting is open and final aggregate totals immediately after voting closes. Candidate percentages use ballots cast, not expected submissions or total selections; multi-select percentages may exceed 100%. Closing never reveals results to Presentation. The administrator explicitly reveals or hides closed results for Presentation, while administrator result access remains available. Closed voting rounds combine sorted results, election decision controls, and elected-candidate response status/actions in one default workspace; Audit remains separate with prior decision revisions. Open and Draft polls keep their Results, Decisions, and Audit tabs.
- Candidate current status is derived from append-only, poll-linked decision events. Revising a decision supersedes its earlier event for the current summary while preserving the original poll decision, audit trail, ballots, and vote totals.
- Voting polls may be created from the approved nomination roster for round one, or from the most recent completed voting poll for later rounds. Another completed voting poll may be selected explicitly. Advanced candidates are preselected; Elected and Declined candidates are excluded by default. Manual selection changes are confirmed before a source change replaces them. Candidate names become immutable poll snapshots after draft creation, and ballot selection limits cannot exceed the selected roster.
- Voters are anonymous guests. Submissions are restricted to an open intake or poll, ballots are recorded without identity, and a browser tab session can submit once per poll. A separate tab or cleared session storage can bypass this simulation-only duplicate restriction.
- Closing a poll stops submissions and immediately makes final aggregate results available to the administrator without publishing them to Presentation. An administrator explicitly reveals or hides closed results for Presentation. Aggregate participation visibility is a separate administrator control and may be shown on Presentation while voting is open.
- Administrators can record election decisions (Pending, Advanced, Elected, Eliminated), add notes, and save revised decisions. Candidate responses (Awaiting response, Accepted, Declined) are separate append-only events available only for elected candidates; finalized positions remain read-only except for an approved later decline. Each decision revision preserves its administrator identity and timestamp in poll history and the activity audit.
- Presentation is read-only and shows the active session position, poll/intake instructions, QR and voter link, optional aggregate participation, and closed aggregate poll results only after explicit administrator reveal. Closing voting alone never publishes results. Neither voter identities nor individual ballots are displayed.
- There are no automatic election, majority, tie-break, cutoff, replacement, or next-round rules. Outcomes are entirely manual. Historical polls retain their ballot/configuration snapshots and decision revisions; changes to current candidate statuses do not rewrite historic poll snapshots.

## State and limits

State is stored in this browser's localStorage and synchronized across same-origin tabs using BroadcastChannel and the storage event. Nomination limits and voting duplicate restrictions are prototype simulations scoped to browser session storage; they are not identity or eligibility controls and can be bypassed with another browser session. State is not shared across browsers or devices, and there is no server, authentication, authorization, encrypted transport, durable audit service, or ballot secrecy guarantee against browser access. Reset demo clears this prototype's local data. The QR/link points to the local Vite origin, so scanning requires the device to reach that host.

## UX policy note

This approved prototype intentionally differs from current product docs/policies by allowing the administrator to show aggregate participation on the audience presentation. Current product docs say neither viewer exposes participation status. Voting-result visibility now matches the clarified policy: the administrator sees live and final aggregate results; the administrator separately reveals closed results to Presentation, and closing never auto-publishes them. Those product documents are unchanged. This app demonstrates prototype-only UX behavior, not production policy or API behavior.
